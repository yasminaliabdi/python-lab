My Budget Tracker

Track your income and expenses in one place.

📖 How to use this tracker

1. Enter the expense name in the "Expense Name" field.

2. Enter the amount you spent in the "Amount" field.

3. Select a category from the dropdown menu.

4. Click the "Add Expense" button to add it to your list.

5. Your expenses will appear in the table below.
